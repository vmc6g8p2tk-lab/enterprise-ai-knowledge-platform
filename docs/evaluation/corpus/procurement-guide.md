# Procurement guide

## Purchase approvals
Purchases above 5000 currency units require approval from the department head and Finance before an order is placed. Smaller purchases still need the cost center owner's approval. Splitting one order into several smaller orders to avoid the threshold is prohibited. The request includes the expected amount, business reason and proposed supplier.

## Vendor security review
Any supplier that will process customer data must complete a security review before contract signature. The requester submits the vendor questionnaire and a data flow summary to Security. Security records approval or conditions in the vendor register. The contract cannot substitute for this review, and the review must be repeated when processing scope changes substantially.

## Contract retention
Signed supplier contracts are stored in the legal repository for seven years after expiry. The contract owner records the expiry date and renewal notice period. Legal approves any early deletion request. Draft contracts stay in the restricted collaboration workspace until signed. The seven-year rule here applies to signed supplier contracts, not customer call recordings.

## Expense reimbursement
Approved business expenses must be submitted within 45 days of purchase with an itemized receipt. Finance may ask for a cost center code or travel approval record. Personal purchases are not reimbursable even if made during a business trip. Reimbursement timing begins when a complete claim is received, not when the employee first opens a draft.
