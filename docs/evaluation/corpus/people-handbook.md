# People handbook

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Requests go through the staff portal and need manager approval before travel is booked. Unused days may roll over only up to five days into the next year. The People team answers questions about balance corrections. This paragraph defines the annual leave rule; it does not define contractor benefits.

## Probation
New employees have a three-month probation period. A manager schedules a review meeting during the final two weeks and records the outcome in the staff portal. An extension requires written approval from the People team. The review considers role expectations and documented feedback, rather than a single test score. The three-month period applies to new employees in this handbook.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after their manager approves a recurring schedule. Teams must keep at least one shared office day for collaboration. A temporary exception for caregiving or travel requires a separate manager decision. Remote days do not change the employee's usual working hours or the incident reporting responsibilities described elsewhere.

## Learning budget
Each full-time employee has a yearly learning budget of 1200 currency units. A course purchase needs manager approval before payment, and the receipt must be uploaded within 30 days. The budget cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another employee. The People team maintains the policy and can clarify whether a proposed course is work related.
