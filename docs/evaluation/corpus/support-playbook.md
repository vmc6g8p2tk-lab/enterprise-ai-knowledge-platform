# Support playbook

## First response
Priority-one support tickets receive a first human response within 15 minutes. A support agent confirms the impact and assigns an incident lead before proposing a fix. Automated acknowledgements do not count as the human first response. The ticket timeline is the source of truth for measuring this target. Lower priority tickets use a different schedule not listed here.

## Escalation
If a priority-one issue remains unresolved after 60 minutes, the support lead escalates to the on-call engineering lead. The escalation includes the ticket ID, customer impact, mitigation tried and next update time. The engineering lead acknowledges within ten minutes. Support continues customer communication while engineering investigates the technical cause.

## Customer updates
During a priority-one incident, support sends affected customers an update every 30 minutes until mitigation is complete. Each update states current impact, action in progress and the next expected update time. Speculative root causes should not be presented as facts. The incident lead approves the final resolution notice after service is stable.

## Satisfaction survey
A customer satisfaction survey is sent 24 hours after a support ticket is closed. The survey asks for a one-to-five rating and optional written feedback. Responses are stored with the ticket record for service improvement. Agents do not alter ratings, and the playbook does not promise a specific satisfaction score for any month.
